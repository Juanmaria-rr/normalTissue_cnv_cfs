What limits these results

Exploratory

No primary test was declared before the pilot; HS68 was picked from 198 tests (q perm. global 0.079).

Few events

The strongest row rests on 10 events; per-donor expectations are below 1.

Clustering

Clones and recurrent regions make the exact p optimistic; the permutation p is the reference.

Resolution and context

Intervals are as wide as the sites; catalogues and RT come from other cell types.

Bins are not independent replications: 200 kb keeps only the deepest cells and intervals widen at 1 Mb. The cells500 and refined variants separate resolution from cell selection. Only breaks that leave a copy-number change are detected.
